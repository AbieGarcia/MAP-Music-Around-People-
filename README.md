# MAP — Music Around People

## About the Project

MAP is a social music discovery app designed to connect people through their musical interests. It allows users to share what they are currently listening to,
discover other users' music preferences, and connect with people who have similar tastes.

## Key Features

- Interactive Music Map: Share the song you're currently listening to on an interactive map.
- Music Discovery: Explore what other users are listening to, whether they are nearby or anywhere around the world.
- Music-Based Matching: Swipe to find users with similar tastes.
- User Profiles: Create an account and share your musical identity with other users.

## Technologies

 - TypeScript
 - Ionic React
 - Firebase
 - Deezer API
